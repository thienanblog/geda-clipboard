Geda Clipboard

Privacy Policy

Geda Clipboard collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. Everything it records stays on your Mac.

Last updated: 11 August 2026. This policy covers the macOS application Geda Clipboard ("the app"), published by Ân Vũ ("we", "us").

The short version

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network connection of any kind in the Mac App Store build. Your clipboard history is written to a private folder on your own Mac and is never sent anywhere. We cannot see it, and neither can anybody else.

What the app stores, and where

To do its job, the app keeps a history of what you copy. That history is held only on your Mac, inside the sandboxed container macOS gives the app:

~/Library/Containers/com.geda.clipboard/Data/Library/Application Support/geda-clipboard/

WhatWhy it existsWhere it lives
Text you copy So you can find and paste it again history.json, on your Mac
Images you copy Same, plus a small thumbnail for the list blobs/, on your Mac
The name and icon of the app you copied from So each entry shows where it came from history.json and icons.json, on your Mac

Your preferences To remember how you configured the app settings.json, on your Mac

No part of this leaves your computer. There is no cloud sync, no backup service and no telemetry.

What the app deliberately does not record

  • Anything a password manager marks as confidential. The app honours the org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType convention that password managers use to opt out, and skips those copies entirely. This is on by default.
  • Anything marked temporary by the app that produced it. Also on by default.
  • Copies from apps you list as ignored. You can name any application in Preferences and the app will never record a copy from it.

Data collected by us: none

In Apple's terms, the app collects no data. We operate no servers, hold no database, and have no way to receive information from your copy of the app. We do not know that you have installed it.

Third parties

The Mac App Store build bundles no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no third-party framework of any kind. Nothing in the app contacts a third-party service.

Apple provides us with anonymous, aggregated sales and crash statistics through App Store Connect, as it does for every app on the store. That data is collected by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy, not by us, and it does not identify you.

The Mac App Store version does not include the automatic paste and never asks for Accessibility permission. Choosing an entry there copies it and returns you to the app you were working in, so you paste it yourself with ⌘V. Accessibility applies only to the version downloaded directly from this site or installed with Homebrew.

Neither permission sends anything anywhere. Both are optional and can be withdrawn at any time in System Settings.

Your control over the data

  • Delete one entry — select it in the popup and press ⌥⌫.
  • Delete everything — Clear all in the popup, or ⌥⇧⌘⌫.
  • Limit what is kept — Preferences sets the maximum number of entries; older ones are discarded automatically.
  • Remove it all — deleting the app removes its container, and the history with it.

Because we never receive any of it, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anybody, including children under 13. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no user-generated content.
