Geda Clipboard

A menu bar clipboard manager that remembers what you copy, tells you when it does, and pastes any of it back into the app you were working in.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Install with Homebrew

brew install --cask thienanblog/tap/geda-clipboard

What it does

Opens where you are looking

Press ⇧⌘V and the popup appears at the pointer, on whichever display it is on. It can hang under the menu bar icon instead if you prefer.

Tells you what it caught

A notification on every copy and paste, showing the source app and a preview. Either alert can be switched off on its own.

Text and images

Images are kept with thumbnails and stored beside the index rather than inside it, so the history stays quick to load.

Counts repeats instead of duplicating

Copying the same thing again bumps the entry you already have and raises its counter, driven by the system clipboard change counter.

Remembers where it came from

Every entry carries the app it was copied from, with its icon, and the first and last time you copied it.

Pastes back for you

The app you came from is remembered before the popup takes focus, then refocused and sent the paste keystroke. The Mac App Store version refocuses it and leaves the ⌘V to you, because App Store rules reserve the permission a keystroke needs for accessibility features.

Skips what should not be kept

Password managers opt out through a convention the app honours, and you can name any application to ignore.

Stays on your Mac

No account, no sync, no analytics, no network. Read the privacy policy — it is short.

Requirements

macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Windows builds are published alongside each release, though macOS is the tested platform.
